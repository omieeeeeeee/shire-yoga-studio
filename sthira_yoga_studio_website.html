Our Approach

Sthira means steady

Sthira was founded in 2019 with a simple idea: create a space where people can practice without pressure. No performance, no competition. Just honest, consistent practice in a room that feels calm.

Our teachers have trained under traditional lineages in Mysore, Rishikesh, and Pune. We teach what we practice. Classes are small, personal, and adapted to the people in the room.
